# Owner decisions: material first, then prose

For a judgment call that is the owner's (rulings, vocabulary, baselines, scope), present the concrete material (the evidence, or an HTML artefact for anything visual) and a one-line recommendation, one point at a time, and let the owner answer in their own words. Use a question form only when the owner asks for one after seeing the material, or for a binary, low-context choice such as a branch name.
