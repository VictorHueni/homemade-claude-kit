# Owner decisions are discussed in prose, not collected through question forms

Stated preference (2026-09-23, after two rejected multi-question forms on plan rulings): "please let's talk about the open point". When a decision is the owner's to make, present the concrete material and a one-line recommendation in prose, one point at a time, and let the owner answer in their own words. Do not bundle several decisions into a structured question tool; the owner rejects the form to ask for clarification and the decision gets delayed twice.

**How to apply:** for judgment calls on rulings, vocabulary, baselines, or scope, write the evidence (the authored input, the produced output, the text it came from) and a recommendation, then stop and let the owner talk. Reserve the question tool for genuinely binary, low-context choices such as picking a branch name or an install location.

**Refinement (2026-10-10):** the form itself is not the problem; the missing material is. Once the owner has seen the concrete case (an HTML artefact rendering the options with the real assets, or the evidence already discussed in prose), they may ask to answer through the question tool, and that works. A form sent before the material exists still gets rejected with a request for an artefact. So: material first (artefact for anything visual), then a form only if the owner asks for one.

**Why:** the owner decides by reading the concrete case, not by choosing among labels; a form hides the material behind the options and forces a rejection to get at it.
